# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing chocolate sales across products, geographies, and sales teams.  
It delivers **executive-ready insights** into revenue drivers, profit margins, shipment efficiency, and salesperson performance.

## Data Set: https://github.com/AngelRayipudi/Chocolate-Sales-Analysis/blob/main/sample-chocolate-shipments-data-all-Apr-2025.xlsx

## 🔑 Key Insights
**Revenue: $141.49M with **57% profit margin**  
**Top Products: Organic Choco Syrup (11.9M), Caramel Stuffed Bars (10.3M)  
**Profitability Gap:Peanut Butter Cubes (90%) vs. 99% Dark & Pure (36%)  
**Salesperson Impact: Top 10 reps generated $98.6M collectively  
**Global Reach: Strong sales across USA, UK, India, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand  
**Operational Efficiency: Shipments totaled 25K, aligning with sales growth  

## 🛠️ Skills & Tools Applied
**Power BI Visualization: KPI cards, sparklines, geo maps, conditional formatting  
**DAX Functions: Profit %, CY vs PY comparisons, dynamic measures  
**Data Modeling: Star schema for sales, product, and geography tables  
**SQL & Python: Data preprocessing and pipeline optimization  
**Data Storytelling: Translating metrics into actionable insights  
**Operational Analytics: Linking shipments and salesperson performance to business outcomes
